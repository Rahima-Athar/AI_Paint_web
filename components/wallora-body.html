Frequently Asked Questions

How does Wallora AI preserve room details and furniture?

Unlike simplistic photo filters that tint everything, Wallora AI isolates wall planes through edge luminosity segmentation. Furniture textures, natural window sunlight beams, molding trim, and cast shadows remain undisturbed for an authentic paint preview.

What is LRV and why does it matter?

LRV stands for Light Reflectance Value, measured on a scale from 0% (pure black absorbing all light) to 100% (pure reflective white). High-LRV shades (60–80%) brighten dim northern-facing rooms, while lower LRVs (20–40%) create intimate, moody luxury sanctuaries.

What is the difference between Demo Mode and AI Inpainting Provider?

Wallora AI strictly adheres to engineering honesty. In Demo Mode, an ultra-fast client-side luminance-preserving shader delivers real-time previews. When connected to a backend inpainting API (e.g. Replicate or Stability AI via Supabase), full generative boundary inpainting takes place.
